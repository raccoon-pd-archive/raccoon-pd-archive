# Notes — E. Marini

Personal observations, recorded between dispatches. These do not
substitute for the official report.

---

**24 JUL 1998 — 02:40 local**

We have lost contact with Bravo for nineteen hours. The last position
fix put them five kilometers north-northwest of the Spencer estate.
The Bell registered to our unit (N21RA) was ferried in by Brad on
the morning of the 23rd. He is one of the better pilots we have but
the weather over the Arklays has been turning, and the radio
geography out there is unforgiving.

I have signed off on Alpha's deployment for daybreak. Wesker will
brief on the ground.

I've left a sealed envelope with dispatch — instructions for the
clean-up if neither team returns. They are not to be opened by anyone
below my rank.

**24 JUL 1998 — 03:10 local**

Wesker brought a courier. I do not recognize the seal. He has not
shared the contents.

**24 JUL 1998 — 03:55 local**

Alpha away.

— E.M.
